## 2024-05-24 - NetWriter & OdeIntegrator Group Matching Optimization
**Learning:** `NetWriter::write()` and `OdeIntegrator::compileGroups()` iterates over all observables and inside the observable loop, it iterates over all species. Inside the species loop, it parses observable patterns with ANTLR if they aren't in the `parsedObservableCache`. Even though patterns are cached, it still counts matches against every species using `UllmannSGIso` matcher which is very expensive.
**Action:** Re-structure `NetWriter::write()` and `OdeIntegrator::compileGroups()` to pre-parse observable patterns and cache them into an array that can be traversed inside the species loop. This prevents redundant hash map lookups (`cacheIt = parsedObservableCache.find(...)`) inside the inner loop and gave a ~15% speed up on synthetic benchmark.
